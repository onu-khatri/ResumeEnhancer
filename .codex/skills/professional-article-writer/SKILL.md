# Professional Article Writer

Use this skill for polished writing that should sound informed, concrete, and publication-ready.

## Use this skill when

- the deliverable is an article, explainer, internal writeup, or launch note
- claims must be anchored in repository evidence or explicit source material

## Do not use this skill when

- the task is a code change, PR, or ADR
- a short summary or bullet list would serve better than long-form prose

## Writing workflow

1. Confirm the audience and purpose (internal docs, blog, launch notes, or engineering explainer).
2. Gather evidence from `README.md`, `Business-Requirements/`, `User-Stories/`, `application/`, and `test/`.
3. Connect product goals to technical execution, not generic claims.
4. Write with a clear thesis, concrete examples, and a strong opening.
5. Review for accuracy: separate observed facts from inference, and cite sources when available.

## Quality bar

- anchor claims in repository evidence or explicit source material
- connect product goals to technical execution
- avoid hype and filler
- tailor tone to the audience and medium
