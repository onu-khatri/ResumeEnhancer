# Research — Job Tracker Board And Application Follow-Up Workspace

## Research Basis

The platform research showed that resume builders are increasingly expanding into adjacent job-search tools. The clearest public signal is Novorésumé's job tracker, which appears as a Kanban-style premium capability on the homepage and pricing page. It is a retention surface and a premium differentiator, not a day-one must-have.

## What The Research Means

- Job tracking extends the platform from "document creation" into **"application management"** — a natural retention surface for premium users.
- The Kanban-style board (stages as columns, cards are draggable) is the proven UX pattern; simple and visual.
- Because competitors tie it to premium (Basic = 3 cards; Premium = unlimited), it acts as a secondary monetization surface.
- Card limits in free tier are explicit — users know exactly when they hit the wall.
- The tracker stays **personal** (not team/recruiter), keeping scope narrow and manageable.

## Requirement Interpretation

- MVP should stay narrow: user-owned cards, basic stages (saved, applied, interviewing, offer, rejected, archived), related resume association, and simple notes/dates.
- Avoid overbuilding an enterprise CRM-like experience.
- The most important frontend outcome is **easy resumption**: a returning user should understand their application state immediately.
- Card-limit messaging must be clear and non-frustrating: show current count, max count, and upgrade CTA.

## Proven Kanban Stage Model (Adapted from Novorésumé + industry standard)

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Saved | Role identified, not yet applied |
| Applied | Application submitted |
| Interviewing | In any interview stage |
| Offer | Offer received |
| Archived | Rejected, withdrawn, or expired |

## Hidden Requirements Inferred

- Card-count tracking per user (free cap, premium unlimited).
- Stage-transition audit (moved from → to, timestamp).
- Resume-to-job-card association (which resume was used for which application).
- Note/reminder storage per card.
- Entitlement gating on card creation.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- The tracker becomes a generic kanban toy with no connection to the resume workflow.
- Users cannot tell which resume version was used for which application.
- Card-limit messaging becomes confusing or hostile if premium policy is not represented clearly.
- Board becomes cluttered for power users if stage management is not scannable.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Build job tracking as a calm, personal workspace extension — not a heavy CRM.
- Connect it lightly to documents (resume association) and premium policy.
- Keep follow-up clarity and stage progression as the primary UX goals.
- 3-card free / unlimited premium is a clean monetization pattern; evaluate if it fits our plan model.

## Open Questions

- Should job tracker be a premium-only feature, or a freemium feature with card limits?
- How many stages in MVP (5-6 vs. customizable)?
- Should the tracker support reminders (dates, follow-up nudges)?
- Should job cards support auto-fill from job descriptions pasted for job-match analysis?
