# Research — Pricing Catalog, Checkout Session, And Return Verification Services

## Research Basis

Public research shows that the commercial layer in this category is not simple “pay and unlock” logic. Competitors expose different monetization models, but both require a backend that can keep feature truth, commercial messaging, and post-checkout state aligned.

## Business Interpretation

- Different pricing strategies still require the same backend discipline: one source of plan truth and reliable payment-state verification. Novorésumé's pay-once model and Resume Now's recurring-access language differ commercially, but both need a single canonical plan source and trustworthy payment confirmation.
- Resume-product users are especially sensitive to mismatches between what pricing promises and what the product unlocks. Novorésumé's membership help content covers refund and access-retention questions, showing that users scrutinize whether paying actually unlocks what was promised.
- Checkout-return continuity is not only a UX nicety; it depends on backend correlation between payment and originating task. Restoring the export or AI task after payment requires the backend to remember why the checkout started.

## Requirement Implications

- A dedicated plan-catalog response should feed both public pricing and in-product upgrade surfaces. Novorésumé's plan matrix (page limits, templates, themes, AI, job tracker) must render identically whether shown on the marketing site or in an in-product paywall.
- Checkout attempts must carry structured origin context instead of plain redirect links only. Knowing the originating task (export, AI, analysis) lets the backend restore the exact journey after payment.
- Return verification must normalize provider-specific outcomes into product-safe statuses. Different providers report success, cancel, and pending differently, so the backend must collapse these into stable statuses the frontend can trust.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Frontend hardcodes plan rules differently from entitlement rules. Without a single source, the pricing page may promise features the entitlement system does not grant, the exact mismatch Novorésumé's help content implies users dispute.
- Support cannot explain "why did I pay but still not export?" Without a clear correlation and timeline, support cannot reconstruct the checkout outcome, increasing refund and trust costs.
- Users receive premature success messaging before provider confirmation settles. Showing "premium unlocked" before the provider confirms leads to broken exports and angry users, a trust risk competitors' refund pages suggest is real.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Keep MVP checkout backend focused on canonical plan retrieval, session creation, and return verification. These three capabilities cover the public pricing and upgrade journeys observed across both competitors.
- Defer broader finance/reporting complexity to finance operations and subscription-management stories. Reconciliation and full subscription lifecycle are separate concerns that should not block the initial checkout flow.
