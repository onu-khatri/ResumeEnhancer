# Research — Privacy Request, Data Export, And Account Deletion Initiation Services

## Research Basis

Public privacy and support materials from the selected competitors make it clear that data-rights expectations are part of the product contract in this category. A backend privacy-request service is needed so the frontend can present honest and stable flows rather than vague legal references.

## Requirement Interpretation

- Privacy actions need explicit backend state models with defined lifecycles rather than being handled as one-off operations; the frontend depends on stable, queryable request states to show the user whether their export is processing, their deletion is pending, or their request requires escalation.
- Deletion and export requests may require policy-aware outcomes beyond simple success/failure, including blocked-by-subscription, requires-escalation, and cooling-off states that the backend must model and communicate clearly.
- Support and auditability are part of privacy-service design; every state transition must be recorded, and the service must support both self-service and support-mediated paths so that users who cannot self-serve (due to policy) are routed to help rather than abandoned.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- FE cannot communicate request state honestly.
- Privacy operations become ad hoc and support-heavy.
- Users lose trust in how their personal data is handled.

## Recommended Backend Interpretation

- Model privacy requests explicitly with lifecycle states.
- Keep frontend metadata clear enough to explain what is and is not self-service.
