# Research — Profile, Preferences, And Account Settings Experience

## Why This Story Exists

Public support and privacy signals across the selected competitors indicate that trust and self-service matter in this product category. Users need to manage their account, preferences, and related support/privacy actions without relying exclusively on customer service. That means profile and settings are part of the real product, even if they are less glamorous than the builder.

## Business Interpretation

- Profile and settings are trust-building surfaces; when users see a polished settings area with clear controls, they perceive the platform as mature and legitimate, which is critical in a category where users entrust their career documents to the product.
- Privacy and support discoverability affect perceived legitimacy — if privacy options and support entry points are hidden or absent, users may assume the platform is not serious about data rights or customer care, eroding willingness to subscribe and continue using the product.
- Self-service settings reduce support burden and improve user confidence by letting users fix common issues (name typos, preference changes, locale adjustments) instantly instead of waiting for support ticket resolution.

## Requirement Implications

- The product should expose a clear account/settings area rather than scattering controls across unrelated sections of the builder or dashboard, ensuring users always know where to manage their identity and preferences.
- Basic preferences and account controls should be user-manageable without requiring email or phone contact; both Resume Now and Novorésumé signal that self-service is the expected baseline in this product tier.
- Support and privacy-related entry points belong near settings and account control, not buried in a website footer, so that users experiencing account or data concerns can navigate naturally from the place they think of as "my account" to the help they need.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Users will struggle to manage their identity or preferences.
- Support volume may increase for simple account tasks.
- The platform may feel less mature or less trustworthy.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Focus MVP on profile, preferences, and trust/self-service discoverability.
- Keep settings simple, modular, and clearly separated from billing complexity.
