# Research — User Notifications And Communication Preferences Experience

## Why This Story Exists

Public support, billing, and account signals from the selected competitors imply a meaningful stream of operational product events: signup verification, billing changes, export access, account recovery, and other workflow-relevant status changes. Even though the public sites do not market a “notification center” heavily, the product category still benefits from trustworthy communication flows and user-managed preferences.

## Business Interpretation

- This category benefits from operational trust communications even when marketing pages do not foreground notifications; both Resume Now and Novorésumé signal reliability through billing receipts, export confirmations, and account reminders rather than through a flashy notification feature.
- Export, billing, AI, and account workflows all create states users should be informed about; each of these workflows has a completion, failure, or confirmation moment where a missed notification could cause user confusion or a perceived platform failure.
- Preference control helps differentiate useful communication from spam; giving users granular opt-outs for non-critical categories reduces the perception that the platform is noisy while preserving the mandatory operational messages that protect the user.

## Requirement Implications

- Notifications should be tied to real product states, not generic engagement; events like export completion, payment success/failure, verification reminders, and account-state changes carry real operational meaning, while promotional nudge messages would dilute trust in the notification system.
- Preference management belongs near settings but the read/action experience belongs in the shell, so users discover notifications where they occur (a bell icon or banner) but adjust their communication choices from the settings area where they expect configuration controls.
- BE event taxonomy and FE presentation need early alignment; without a shared category vocabulary (document, billing, account, export, verification), the frontend cannot render consistent grouping, icons, or mandatory-vs-optional distinctions and would have to infer meaning from ad-hoc strings.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Important events become hidden in email or impossible to find later.
- Users may feel surprised by billing or export outcomes.
- The team may mix mandatory operational notices with optional marketing signals.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Start with operational notifications and preference basics.
- Avoid overbuilding campaign-style communication in early phases.
