# Research — Public Template Discovery, Example Browsing, And Funnel Entry Experience

## Research Basis

The public research made one pattern very clear: in this category, public discovery pages are not passive marketing. They are working acquisition funnels. Both selected references invest heavily in public template, example, and category pages because job seekers often decide whether to trust the product before they ever see the editor.

## What The Research Means

- Search and category entry matter before product use begins. Resume Now's ATS-friendly category page and Novorésumé's resume-examples blog content show that visitors frequently arrive through search and role-specific category routes, so those landing experiences must be first-class.
- Visitors need relevance cues such as ATS-safe, role fit, or design style before they are ready to sign up. Resume Now segments templates by ATS-friendliness specifically to reduce hesitation, which is a signal that trust labels are conversion levers.
- Public discovery and onboarding continuity must work together, or acquisition intent is lost between page view and account creation. Both competitors route a chosen template into their start flow, implying the selected asset must survive the signup hand-off.

## Requirement Interpretation

- Developers should treat public galleries as intentional product funnels, not static marketing leftovers. Resume Now's large template library and Novorésumé's gallery both function as acquisition infrastructure, not decoration.
- Filter state and selected-asset context should survive transition into signup or start flow. The value of a filtered or selected asset is lost if the user must re-find it after registering.
- Cards and detail pages need practical explanation, not only decoration. Competitor cards communicate why an asset is relevant (ATS-safe, role fit), which is what converts browsing into action.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Teams may build generic public grids with no strong continuation logic. Without deliberate CTA design, galleries become dead ends that undercut the acquisition intent the competitors clearly invest in.
- Search-landing visitors may lose context when they enter signup. If a visitor arrives on a role-specific example page but lands in a generic signup, the relevance that drew them in is discarded.
- Public category labels may diverge from actual template or ATS behavior. Resume Now's ATS-friendly segment works because the label matches product truth; divergence would erode trust and invite support complaints.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Start with templates, examples, categories, and strong CTA continuity. These four surfaces match the proven acquisition pattern seen in both Resume Now and Novorésumé.
- Keep authoring/CMS complexity out of this story. Content authoring is a separate backend concern and should not block the discovery funnel.
- Optimize for relevance, trust, and funnel continuity. Every layout decision should serve the visitor's confidence-building and the hand-off into the product.
