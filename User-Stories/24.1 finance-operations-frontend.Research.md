# Research — Finance And Billing Operations Interface

## Why This Story Exists

The public competitors expose meaningful billing complexity: recurring terms, refund conditions, cancellations, premium expiry, and self-service account actions. That implies internal finance/billing operations beyond simple checkout success, especially for support and trust-sensitive issue handling.

## Business Interpretation

- Finance and billing support visibility is part of maintaining trust in a paid product, since recurring-billing issues are among the most common and most sensitive support topics.
- Operators need billing truth separate from user-facing summaries, so they can see failed payments, refund states, and entitlement mismatches that the user view obscures.
- A read-focused internal finance interface is a practical MVP, matching the industry pattern of starting with operational visibility before building action tooling.

## Requirement Implications

- Billing operations should have dedicated internal visibility beyond generic audit views, because finance teams need structured, filterable records rather than scattered log entries.
- Finance and support roles may overlap but should not be identical, since support agents generally need narrower access than finance operators.
- Refund and subscription anomaly review need explicit data models, so payment, refund, and subscription states are clearly distinguishable in the UI.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Billing support cases remain slow and ad hoc, as operators piece together commercial truth from fragmented sources.
- Internal teams depend too heavily on raw provider dashboards, which rarely map cleanly to the product's own subscription and entitlement model.
- Product truth and provider truth become harder to reconcile operationally, increasing the risk of incorrect refunds or access decisions.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Start with searchable review and summary visibility, which delivers the highest diagnostic value with the least risk.
- Add controlled finance actions later if necessary, once review patterns stabilize and the need for mutation is proven.
