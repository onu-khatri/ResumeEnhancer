# Research — Admin Analytics And Audit Visibility Workspace

## Why This Story Exists

The public products reveal enough operational complexity to justify internal analytics and audit visibility even though those tools are not publicly exposed. Billing, privacy, premium gating, account management, and support operations all require some internal way to understand product health and sensitive changes.

## Business Interpretation

- Internal analytics and audit are part of operating a trustworthy paid platform, giving operators the visibility needed to answer "what changed?" and "what is going wrong?".
- Product, support, and compliance functions all benefit from visibility into sensitive actions and health signals, since each role diagnoses a different slice of platform behavior.
- A read-only operator workspace is a reasonable early step before deeper internal tooling, mirroring how observability platforms start with visibility before adding automation.

## Requirement Implications

- Audit and analytics should be modeled as internal product capabilities, not only infrastructure concerns, so they surface in an operator-friendly workspace rather than raw logs.
- FE and BE should expose different access tiers if needed, letting some operators see summaries while others drill into detailed audit events.
- Internal observability should focus on operator usefulness, not just raw volume, prioritizing signal and scannability over exhaustive data.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Sensitive changes become hard to trace, leaving the team without a reliable record of who changed what and when.
- Support and product teams rely on ad hoc database queries, a slow and risky substitute for a governed visibility surface.
- Trust-impacting issues become slower to diagnose, increasing resolution time for billing, access, and privacy incidents.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Focus MVP on summaries plus searchable audit records, which together cover the highest-value operator questions.
- Keep it operationally useful and read-only first, deferring action tooling until visibility patterns are proven.
