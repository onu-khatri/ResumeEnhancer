# Research — Moderation And Abuse-Review Operations Interface

## Why This Story Exists

The selected competitor products expose public uploads, AI tooling, billing, saved accounts, and privacy-sensitive data handling. Those product characteristics imply a trust and safety layer behind the scenes, even if it is not publicly visible. An internal moderation/abuse-review interface becomes increasingly important as platform surface area grows.

## Business Interpretation

- AI, uploads, and account systems create moderation and abuse-review needs even in a career product, mirroring how trust and safety has become standard even for non-social platforms.
- Internal operations need a controlled way to inspect and resolve flagged events, so reviewers can act on signals without accessing unnecessary raw content.
- A minimal trust/safety interface is a reasonable early operational investment, starting small before abuse volume and complexity demand richer tooling.

## Requirement Implications

- Abuse/moderation should be modeled as an internal operational domain, with its own taxonomy, states, and access controls rather than piggybacking on generic admin.
- Category and severity metadata are essential for useful operator review, since they let reviewers triage by urgency and type.
- Privacy and access controls must be especially strong, because moderation data often contains personal or policy-sensitive content.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Suspicious or policy-sensitive activity becomes harder to investigate, as flagged events remain scattered and unstructured.
- Trust/safety work remains ad hoc and engineering-dependent, forcing developers into every review that should be operator-owned.
- Sensitive internal review decisions lack consistency, leading to uneven or undocumented handling of similar cases.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Start with review and status visibility, not heavy action tooling, so operators can inspect and classify before any automation is added.
- Expand as platform volume and abuse complexity justify it, layering detection and bulk actions only when the review foundation is proven.
