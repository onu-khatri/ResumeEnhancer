## [HOME-FE-001] - Public Resume Enhancer Homepage And Builder Entry

**Module:** Public Discovery And Acquisition
**Layer:** Frontend
**Priority:** Must Have
**Persona:** New Visitor, New User, Active Job Seeker
**Dependency:** AUTH-FE-001; IMP-FE-001 for resume-import continuation; ATS-FE-001 for live analysis continuation; public destinations supplied by DISC-FE-001 and BILL-FE-002 where linked
**Source Classification:** Observed, Inferred, Recommended

### User Story

As a job seeker evaluating Resume Enhancer,
I want a credible homepage that shows how my resume can be improved and gives me a clear way to start with my own document,
so that I can confidently begin a resume-improvement journey rather than navigate a generic marketing page.

### Description

This story replaces the current unauthenticated redirect from `/` to `/login` with a responsive public homepage for Resume Enhancer. The page positions resume upload and analysis as the dominant visitor action, communicates the product with realistic but representative interface previews, and repeatedly directs the visitor to the approved start path.

The homepage is an acquisition and product-entry surface. It does not implement file ingestion, parsing, AI analysis, job matching, pricing, or account creation. A hero upload/drop-zone interaction may retain a selected file only through an approved secure continuation contract; it must otherwise route the visitor to onboarding without transmitting or persisting the file.

### Scope

**Included In Scope**

- Public homepage at `/` for unauthenticated visitors and workspace redirect for authenticated visitors.
- Sticky public navigation with approved product, resource, pricing, and sign-in destinations.
- Two-column hero with the approved headline, resume upload-oriented entry control, privacy-supporting copy, and representative analysis preview.
- Social-proof, process, analysis-demo, feature, job-match, before/after, improvement-plan, pricing-preview, FAQ, final-CTA, and footer sections.
- Repeatable, task-oriented CTAs that preserve allowlisted source context and reach working approved routes.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible, screen-reader-accessible public UI and privacy-safe funnel telemetry.

**Out Of Scope**

- Live public upload, malware scanning, parsing, resume storage, or anonymous analysis.
- Account registration, session implementation, onboarding, import review, AI rewriting, analysis reports, job matching, export, or checkout.
- Invented testimonials, ratings, hiring outcomes, customer counts, company affiliations, certifications, or privacy guarantees.
- CMS, A/B testing, campaign management, SEO rendering strategy, and new analytics-provider integration.

### Preconditions

- `AUTH-FE-001` defines the primary public handoff and accepts the allowlisted `homepage` source context.
- `IMP-FE-001` and its backend dependency define whether a selected pre-authentication file can be retained securely through signup. Until then, the hero upload control must not upload or persist a file.
- `ATS-FE-001` owns the live analysis and job-match workspace; homepage previews are representative only.
- Every rendered destination is an approved, implemented internal route. Planned destinations are omitted or presented without navigation.
- Product approves final copy, proof content, file-format language, legal/privacy language, media assets, and page destinations before release.
- Analytics event ownership, consent expectations, and a privacy-safe schema are agreed before production telemetry is enabled.

### Detailed Workflow

1. An unauthenticated visitor opens `/` from a direct link, search result, or campaign.
2. A sticky navigation offers the brand, approved public destinations, sign in, and the primary `Enhance My Resume` CTA.
3. The hero explains the resume-improvement outcome and presents an upload/drop-zone entry control with supported-format guidance only when product policy approves those formats.
4. When the visitor chooses a file or the primary CTA, the page records `homepage` source context and follows the approved onboarding/import continuation. It does not analyze a document on the homepage.
5. The visitor can inspect concise product previews: analysis categories, job-match concepts, before/after bullet examples, improvement-plan priorities, plan comparison, FAQs, and repeated CTAs.
6. A visitor selects an available secondary destination and reaches its working public route; unavailable destinations are not active links.
7. A returning visitor chooses sign in and reaches the sign-in route. An authenticated user who opens `/` is routed to the workspace.

### Functional Requirements

- `/` must render a public homepage for unauthenticated visitors instead of automatically redirecting them to sign in.
- The hero must present the approved headline: "Build a resume that gets you more interviews." It must pair this with concise supporting copy about personalized improvement for content, keywords, formatting, and ATS compatibility, subject to approved claim wording.
- The hero must make the upload-oriented start action visually dominant and provide a non-drag-and-drop equivalent control.
- The page must communicate that its score, ATS, recommendation, job-match, and improvement-plan panels are representative previews until the visitor reaches the applicable product workflow.
- The primary and repeated `Enhance My Resume`, `Upload Resume`, `Analyze My Resume`, and `Match My Resume` actions must use the same approved start/continuation behavior, unless their labels lead to a distinct implemented route.
- The page must show only approved file-format and privacy language. It must not imply secure storage, analysis completion time, or supported formats that the import pipeline does not support.
- The navigation must include Resume Builder, Resume Checker, Templates, Resources, Pricing, Sign In, and the primary CTA only where working destinations are available. The required information architecture must remain visible even when an unavailable item is intentionally non-interactive.
- Homepage sections must remain concise, scannable, and visually ordered around the visitor's next action rather than long-form marketing copy.

### Acceptance Criteria

**AC1 - Public Root And Navigation**
Given an unauthenticated visitor opens `/`
When the route resolves
Then they see a public, sticky-nav homepage with the approved product navigation, sign-in action, and visually primary `Enhance My Resume` CTA instead of a forced sign-in redirect.

**AC2 - Hero Upload Entry**
Given a visitor uses the hero upload/drop-zone control by file selection, drag and drop, or keyboard
When they initiate the action
Then they receive an accessible start handoff with `homepage` source context; and no file is uploaded, stored, or analyzed before an approved import-continuation contract is available.

**AC3 - Supported Format And Privacy Copy**
Given the homepage presents accepted file formats or privacy messaging
When a visitor reads the hero
Then that language matches approved import policy and legal copy; unsupported formats or unverified privacy claims are not displayed.

**AC4 - Product Preview Clarity**
Given a visitor views the hero score panel, analysis demo, job-match panel, before/after examples, or improvement-plan card
When they inspect those surfaces
Then the interface communicates a realistic product outcome without presenting mock scores, keyword counts, or examples as the visitor's actual results.

**AC5 - Required Page Sections**
Given the homepage renders on a supported viewport
When the visitor scrolls through it
Then they can find the approved process, six-feature grid, pricing preview, FAQ, final CTA, and footer, with each section having a concise purpose and a working action where one is shown.

**AC6 - Truthful Proof Content**
Given social proof, logos, ratings, or testimonials are displayed
When the page is prepared for release
Then every item has approved provenance and wording, does not imply an official partnership or hiring guarantee, and is omitted when approval is unavailable.

**AC7 - Returning And Authenticated Visitors**
Given a visitor selects sign in
When they use the action
Then they reach the approved sign-in flow; and given an authenticated user opens `/`, they are routed to the workspace rather than a signup-oriented homepage.

**AC8 - Responsive Accessibility**
Given the homepage is used with keyboard-only navigation, a screen reader, or a mobile viewport
When the visitor reads and operates it
Then headings, landmarks, visible focus, accessible names, drop-zone alternatives, contrast, tap targets, and content reflow meet the project's WCAG 2.2 AA expectations.

**AC9 - Resilience And Observability**
Given non-critical media or analytics delivery fails
When the failure occurs
Then meaningful page copy and every core start/sign-in action remain usable; and given a view or CTA selection succeeds, the defined privacy-safe funnel event is emitted without resume content, credentials, or unnecessary personal data.

### Business Rules

- The homepage prioritizes value before payment. Pricing may be previewed, but the primary journey must offer a usable free-start path.
- Homepage content must frame resume scores, ATS compatibility, job matching, and AI suggestions as guidance, never as a promise of an interview, job offer, or guaranteed ATS outcome.
- The homepage may show an upload-oriented entry control, but live file handling remains owned by `IMP-FE-001` and `IMP-BE-001`. It cannot bypass authentication, authorization, scanning, parsing, retention, or ownership rules.
- Representative UI values, before/after examples, and improvement-plan statuses are illustrative. They must not be presented as user-specific output.
- Source context and destination routing are allowlisted. The homepage must not accept arbitrary return URLs or forward untrusted values into onboarding.
- Public acquisition content and authenticated workspace content remain separate; the homepage must not expose internal, account, resume, or admin data.

### Validation Rules

- A file-select control must accept only formats approved by the import policy. Drag-and-drop and file-picker behavior must reject unsupported files before any handoff attempt.
- Until secure pre-authentication file continuation exists, selected-file metadata must not be persisted in browser storage, analytics, URLs, or logs.
- Primary and secondary CTA destinations must resolve to approved internal routes.
- Every displayed testimonial, logo, rating, testimonial result, and privacy statement needs a recorded approved source before release.
- Decorative previews and visuals must be hidden from assistive technology; informative previews require concise accessible descriptions.

### Error Handling

- If the selected file is unsupported, oversized, or cannot be retained for the approved continuation, explain the condition and offer a working start or template-based alternative without uploading the file.
- If a linked public route is unavailable, do not render it as an active link; keep a working primary path available.
- If non-essential media fails, retain its section's text and CTA without a broken-image-only experience.
- If telemetry fails, do not block navigation or alter the user-visible result.

### Security Considerations

- Never upload, persist, or transmit a resume from the homepage before the approved import contract is available.
- Do not expose resume data, credentials, session tokens, internal identifiers, unpublished content, or personal data in public-page markup or telemetry.
- Validate and allowlist all route, source, campaign, and file-related context before navigation; do not create an open redirect.
- Treat a file name and all campaign/query input as untrusted data. Do not render it unsafely or use it to construct external navigation.

### Analytics / Tracking

- `homepage_upload_entry_started`
- `homepage_secondary_cta_clicked`
- `homepage_sign_in_clicked`
- `homepage_faq_opened`

### API Dependencies

- No new homepage API is required for static content and representative product previews.
- Existing authentication/session state determines whether `/` renders the homepage or routes the visitor to the workspace.
- `AUTH-FE-001` supplies the onboarding entry route.
- `IMP-FE-001` and `IMP-BE-001` supply any approved import-continuation contract.
- `ATS-FE-001` and `ATS-BE-001` supply live analysis and job-match results after the visitor reaches the product workflow.

### Data Requirements

- Approved static copy, visual assets, FAQ entries, plan-preview content, and legal/footer links.
- Approved illustrative analysis, job-match, before/after, and improvement-plan data clearly separated from live user data.
- Enumerated CTA identifiers and `homepage` source attribution.
- Privacy-safe analytics metadata such as page path, CTA identifier, and approved campaign identifier.

### UX Notes

- Use a light professional SaaS visual direction: light neutral surfaces, a restrained blue/indigo accent, rounded cards, subtle borders/shadows, and a spacious content width near 1200px.
- Use a purposefully selected modern typeface compatible with the product design system; do not let typography or decorative illustration compete with the resume-improvement action.
- Use realistic UI mockups rather than decorative AI/chatbot imagery. The hero preview should make the product value understandable more quickly than adjacent copy.
- Repeat the primary action naturally at the hero, analysis demo, job-match section, pricing preview, and final CTA. Each repetition must retain consistent handoff behavior.

### Definition Of Ready For Engineering

- `AUTH-FE-001` onboarding entry contract and `homepage` source-context behavior are available.
- Product approves final page copy, illustrative data, visual assets, file-format policy, and privacy/legal claims.
- The initial public destination map identifies which navbar/footer links are implemented, hidden, or non-interactive.
- Product decides whether a selected pre-authentication file may be retained through onboarding; if yes, `IMP-FE-001`/`IMP-BE-001` provide the secure continuation contract.
- Analytics schema, consent expectations, and provider ownership are defined.

### Definition of Done

- `/` renders the approved responsive, accessible homepage for unauthenticated visitors while preserving authenticated workspace handoff.
- The sticky navigation, hero/upload entry, representative previews, six-feature grid, pricing preview, FAQ, final CTA, and footer are implemented according to approved content.
- All rendered CTAs and links reach working approved destinations, with homepage-to-onboarding attribution covered by targeted frontend tests.
- Representative data cannot be mistaken for live analysis, and the page does not perform unapproved public file handling.
- Release proof content is source-approved, or unavailable logos/testimonials/ratings are omitted.
- Accessibility, responsive behavior, non-critical-media fallback, and telemetry-failure behavior are covered by targeted tests.
