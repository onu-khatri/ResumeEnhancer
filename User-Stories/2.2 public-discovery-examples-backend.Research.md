# Research — Public Discovery Catalog And Funnel-Metadata Services

## Research Basis

Competitor research shows that public templates and examples are not incidental content. They are core acquisition surfaces. That means the backend needs deliberate public-safe catalog services rather than reusing internal inventory structures blindly.

## Backend Interpretation

- Public and internal content views have different contracts and different safety requirements. Resume Now's public template and example catalogs expose only presentation-ready metadata, while the content administration stories imply a separate, richer internal inventory behind them.
- Public categories and CTA targets need to align with onboarding and product-start flows. Novorésumé's gallery funnels visitors into its own start flow, so the backend CTA metadata must match the actual onboarding entry points.
- A stable public catalog helps marketing and product stay consistent. Because both Resume Now and Novorésumé use their catalogs as long-lived SEO surfaces, the public contract must remain stable over time.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Internal admin fields may leak to public responses. Reusing internal inventory structures directly risks exposing draft, moderation, and admin-only data to anonymous visitors.
- Search and category pages may depend on brittle ad hoc transformations. Without dedicated projections, the frontend builds fragile transformations that break as the internal model evolves.
- Funnel continuity breaks because public assets do not carry usable continuation metadata. If assets lack structured CTA targets, visitors cannot be routed cleanly into the same start flows Novorésumé and Resume Now provide.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Build dedicated public catalog projections for templates, examples, and categories. These projections mirror the public-safe catalogs implied by Resume Now's and Novorésumé's discovery surfaces.
- Keep payloads lightweight, cache-friendly, and publication-safe. Lightweight public projections support the fast, cacheable delivery those public galleries depend on for SEO and performance.
