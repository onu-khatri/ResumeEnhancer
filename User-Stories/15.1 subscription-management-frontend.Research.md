# Research — Subscription And Billing Management Experience

## Why This Story Exists

The existing export/billing stories cover the monetization moment, but public competitor evidence shows that billing clarity continues after checkout. Users need to understand renewal, expiry, cancellation, and what remains available after plan changes. That makes subscription management a real product surface rather than an afterthought.

## Business Interpretation

- Subscription clarity is part of trust and churn prevention — Resume Now's contact-us page references dashboard cancellation and billing help expectations, which signals that users frequently seek support for billing questions that could be answered by a well-designed self-service page. Novorésumé's help article on membership questions and refunds similarly shows that users need clear post-purchase information, not just checkout-time messaging.
- The two competitors embody two strategies: Novorésumé is **pay-once, time-boxed** (explicit "no auto-renewal," 14-day refund, data retained after expiry); Resume Now is **recurring subscription** (renewal language, cancellation obligations). Both still need a coherent account-management surface — the difference is what that surface must communicate. A pay-once model primarily explains expiry dates and post-expiry data access; a subscription model must additionally explain renewal dates, cancellation procedures, and what happens after cancellation.
- The product must explain not just how to upgrade, but what happens after payment, after expiry, and after cancellation — Resume Now's terms of use describe recurring billing and cancellation behavior, indicating that users who don't understand these terms upfront become support cases later. The billing management page is the product's opportunity to preempt those support cases.
- A pay-once model reduces renewal/cancellation complexity (lower support risk); a subscription model requires clearer renewal disclosure, cancellation flows, and proration messaging. Our choice must be deliberate — see `billing-entitlements-domain.BR.md`. Regardless of the model chosen, the frontend must render the consequences of that model in plain, user-facing language.

## Requirement Implications

- Billing state needs a dedicated settings-area representation — the subscription management page is not a checkout flow or a pricing page; it is an account-settings area that shows the user their current standing. Competitor evidence shows that users expect to find billing information in a dedicated account section, not buried inside an export or upgrade flow.
- FE messaging must align tightly with BE billing truth — Novorésumé's help content explains premium expiry and refund conditions in precise terms, and Resume Now's terms of use detail cancellation behavior. If the frontend paraphrases these rules incorrectly, users will act on wrong information and become support cases. The backend must be the single source of truth for all billing messaging.
- Support entry points belong near billing actions — Resume Now's public support page references dashboard cancellation and billing help, indicating that users often need human assistance even with self-service tools available. The billing management page should include a visible help/support link so that users who get stuck can escalate without leaving the page.

## Risks If Under-Specified

- Users misunderstand renewal or expiry — Resume Now's recurring billing language and Novorésumé's time-boxed access model both create expectations about what happens next. If our billing page doesn't clearly state whether the user will be charged again, when their access ends, and what data they retain, users will either be surprised by charges or lose confidence in the product's transparency.
- Support burden increases due to preventable billing confusion — every billing question that reaches support is one that the self-service page failed to answer. Competitor support pages (Resume Now's contact-us, Novorésumé's Intercom help) exist in part because their billing UIs don't fully explain plan consequences. A well-designed billing page should reduce support ticket volume measurably.
- Product trust falls even if the checkout itself works — a smooth checkout creates a positive first impression, but a confusing billing management experience erodes trust over time. Users who feel uncertain about their plan status are less likely to upgrade and more likely to churn, even if the product's actual billing behavior is fair.

## Recommended Scope Interpretation

- Focus MVP on current plan, status, next step, and allowed self-service actions.
- Keep deeper finance features out of scope initially.
